# Relaunch shot list — 30 minutes, one phone

The photo-dump carousel failed on **sourcing, not prompting**: 8 of its 9
slides were real photos, so reprompting reached exactly one. The pool
(53 files) is portraits and lifestyle — dog, lake selfie, family walk,
award dinner, car selfie. It reads as a camera roll because it *is* one.

What's missing is the realtor-page money shot: **the person IN the work.**
Not more faces, not more work — faces *in* work context. That's what makes
a local-expert page read as expert instead of personal.

## Shoot this (golden hour, ~6:30–7:15pm Phoenix in August)

**Vertical (4:5 or 9:16). Plain shirt — no company marks, see the block
below. Someone else holds the phone for anything with your face; a
tripod + timer works.**

### Tier 1 — needs no job site (shoot these even if nothing else happens)

1. **The closer shot.** You, straight to camera, natural expression, outdoors, AZ light. Not a pitch face. This is the "hi, I'm the guy" frame and it's the one the whole page hangs on.
2. **Truck + house.** Your vehicle parked on a residential street with S-tile roofs and gravel yards behind it. Wide.
3. **The street.** A row of Arizona homes, a couple with panels, no people. Pure context.
4. **Tailgate / gear.** You at the truck bed, gear visible, mid-motion — looking at what you're doing, not at the lens.
5. **Your own bill or usage screen.** Your phone or paper, your hand, real numbers. Never a customer's.
6. **Golden-hour array.** Any panels catching low sun. Let the light do the work.
7. **The walk-up.** You approaching a front door or driveway from behind/side, mid-stride. Anonymous, human, and it reads as the job.

### Tier 2 — if you can get to a site or a roof

8. **Hands on equipment.** Close, no face — meter, panel edge, rail, tool in hand.
9. **On the roof, wide.** You small in frame, roofline and Valley behind you.
10. **Meter / disconnect close-up.** Where the money actually happens.
11. **Working, not posed.** Anything where you're clearly mid-task and unaware of the camera.

**12–15 frames total is plenty.** Shoot more than you need of #1 — the
straight-to-camera shot is the hardest to get right and the most valuable.

## Hard blocks

- **No other company's branding.** Two existing slides carry real marks (a branded tote, a polo logo) and are hard-blocked regardless of anything else — a page relaunching you as an independent local expert cannot show another company's identity on your chest. Plain shirt for the shoot.
- **No identifiable customer home** without written permission.
- **No third-party faces** without clearance (already cleared for the existing pool).
- **Real photos only** in the proof lane — the AI candids are punctuation, never the substance.

## What this unlocks

With 12–15 new frames, the 2–3 genuinely good existing shots (the rooftop
pair, the on-roof tablet) stop carrying the post and become punctuation.
The carousel becomes: strong real opener → work context → the city →
personal beat → straight-to-camera closer. That's a realtor page.
