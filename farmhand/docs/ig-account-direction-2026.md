## What the grid is for

Two audiences see this page:
1. **Customers** (primary) — must find AZ-specific and general solar education
   that is valuable to any viewer, plus a person they'd trust.
2. **Industry peers** (secondary) — they get Stories, not posts.

## The two allowed post lanes

Every grid post is one of these. Nothing else posts.

**Lane 1 — Lifestyle** (posts, carousels, reels)
- Shows what this kind of job affords: time, travel, training, the dog, AZ life.
- Face-on-frame encouraged; aspirational but grounded.
- Not a persona performance — no invented routines.

**Lane 2 — Educational solar** (posts, carousels, reels)
- General solar education, or AZ-specific (APS/SRP, TOU plans, rates,
  heat/datacenter demand — source from the az-*.md research docs in this folder).
- Consultant register, never sales register.

## Hard exclusions from the grid → Stories only

- Commission numbers, earnings screenshots, "X sales today" tallies
- Recruiting / join-my-team / exec-sales content
- Team blitz or contest content

Rationale (his): a customer viewing the page must never hit content that reads
as a sales-org recruiting operation. Industry followers who want the wins watch
Stories.
