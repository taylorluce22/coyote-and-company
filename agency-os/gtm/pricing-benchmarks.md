Researched 2026-08-01 (five parallel research passes; every figure traces to a
source URL — nothing here is invented). Purpose: fill the term sheet's blanks
(`$[X,XXX]` retainer / `$[XXX]` meeting fee / `[XX]%` commission) with numbers
the founder can defend in a negotiation, and answer "why not commission-only?"
with market evidence rather than opinion.

## 4. Commission-only: what it really costs

The founder asked "why not just a percentage?" The market's answer: you can,
and the price of that choice is roughly **a doubled-or-more rate plus survival
risk you don't control**.

- Commission-only rates run **2–4× the hybrid equivalent** because the seller
  carries all income risk ([CommissionCrowd](https://blog.commissioncrowd.com/posts/8-Reasons-Independent-Commission-Only-Sales-Reps-Earn-More-Than-Salaried-Employees));
  compensation-design guidance puts pure-commission at **20–50%** ([low-confidence single source]) and commission-only device reps at 10–25%+.
- Commission-only fails structurally when the sales cycle exceeds 1–2 months —
  semi-complex B2B runs ~4 months top-of-funnel to close, and our first dollar
  arrives only after the supplier closes, invoices, AND collects ([YourSales](https://yoursales.com/outsourced-commission-only-b2b-sales-good-bad-ugly/)).
- The agency-side risk is the client, not the work: under-reported sales,
  slow collections, insolvency — all outside our control, and our commission
  base is literally the supplier's self-reported monthly collections
  ([Business of Agency](https://businessofagency.com/p/commission-only-deals-put-your-agency-at-risk)).
- Buyer-side signal: agencies offering commission-only are "telling you they
  can't afford the upfront investment" — the retainer funds the data, tooling,
  and qualification work ([Belkins](https://belkins.io/blog/lead-generation-pricing)).
  Our retainer funds exactly that: license verification with evidence.

## 5. Compliance reinforcement surfaced by the research

DOJ actively prosecutes percentage-of-claims deals with 1099 marketers where
**federal healthcare programs** are billed (e.g., a $31M judgment in a
telehealth/pharmacy marketing case — [Frier Levitt](https://www.frierlevitt.com/articles/doj-kickback-judgment-telehealth-pharmacy-compliance-risks/),
[Newman Law](https://jeffnewmanlaw.com/in-healthcare-paying-commissions-to-1099-sales-reps-violates-the-anti-kickback-statute-will-labs-and-pharmacies-ever-learn/)).
This is precisely why the term sheet's **federal-program exclusion** (no
commission on any federally billable sale, supplier certifies monthly, audit
right, retroactive carve-out) is marked non-negotiable. The research validates
the clause; nothing found argues for loosening it. Commission tails in rep
agreements also normally run 30–90 days on pipeline orders — our 24-month
introduced-account tail is a different (revenue-share) concept and is the
generous end of finder norms; expect suppliers to negotiate it, and trade tail
length before trading rate.

## 6. Runway math — what commission-only actually costs us

The founder's counterargument to the hybrid (2026-08-01): *no track record
means pay-on-outcome IS the wedge — suppliers with no reason to trust us will
pay when we produce, not before.* The "commission-only signals weakness"
finding (§4) describes agencies with payroll. We have scripts and SaaS. So the
deciding question is burn, not doctrine.

**Monthly cash burn, all-in** (base from `docs/07-stack.md` §Estimated
monthly cost, which itemizes Airtable Team $24 / Make Core $18 / Instantly
$37–97 / MillionVerifier ~$10 PAYG / Google Workspace $14 / Dropbox Sign $20 /
QBO $38 / Cal.com $0 / domains ~$10):

| Item | $/month |
|---|---|
| Platform stack (07-stack.md MVP estimate) | $180–260 |
| Email finder for the 3,200 contacts (Hunter/Apollo-class — the one item the stack estimate omits; droppable after the list is filled) | $0–49 |
| **Total burn** | **≈ $200–310/mo** |

One-time setup: ~$50 (three domains + the launch batch of MillionVerifier
credits). LinkedIn Sales Navigator ($99/mo) deliberately excluded — supplier
outreach is eight named people; free LinkedIn covers it.

**Cash needed to reach first commission income:**

| Scenario | Cash out of pocket |
|---|---|
| First collection at day 90 (fast: supplier signs wk 2–4, buyer meetings wk 4–8, close + invoice + net-30 collection) | **≈ $650–$980** |
| First collection at day 180 (slow but survivable) | ≈ $1,250–$1,900 |

Break-even illustration (the order size is an ASSUMPTION for scale, not a
sourced figure): one clinic account ordering $2,500–$5,000/month of compounded
preparations at an 18% commission pays $450–$900/month — a single modest
account covers the entire platform burn. The real cost of commission-only is
founder time at risk, not cash.

**What pure commission gives up — priced:** the qualified-meeting fee is the
earliest possible revenue (meetings happen weeks 4–8; collections land day
90+). Dropping it moves first income out by ~2–3 months AND removes the early
signal that the supplier values what we deliver. But a meeting fee is ALSO
pay-on-outcome — a held, license-verified meeting is a produced outcome. So
the founder's principle ("paid when we produce") doesn't require pure
commission; it requires no retainer.

## 7. The menu (founder's decision — priced so every option is fair)

**Lead with B. It is the founder's wedge, correctly priced.**

| | Structure | When money arrives | Why a supplier picks it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A — established-agency pricing** | $3,000/mo retainer (frame: *market development fee*, MANA's concept; 50% creditable against commissions) + $350/qualified meeting held + **8–10%** of collected revenue, 24-mo tail | Month 1 | Cheapest at volume; for suppliers who want us committed and exclusive-ish |
| **B — pay-on-outcome (LEAD OFFER)** | No retainer. **$350–400 per qualified meeting held + 15%** of collected revenue, 24-mo tail | First meetings (weeks 4–8) | "You pay only when we produce: a verified meeting, then revenue." The no-track-record wedge, with an early revenue checkpoint |
| **C — pure commission** | **18–20%** of collected revenue, nothing else. 24-mo tail non-negotiable | Day 90+ | Zero risk until revenue lands. Priced at the market's 2–4× commission-only premium (§4) |

Non-negotiable riders on B and C (they're what make the wait survivable, per
§4's agency-risk findings): monthly collections reporting with audit right
(already in the term sheet), 2-business-day introduction-response SLA, and a
mutual 90-day option to renegotiate structure once real volume data exists.

**Floors:** commission never below 8% with a retainer, 12% with meeting fees
only, 15% pure — below those we're donating the risk premium the entire
market charges. Concede tail length before rate.
