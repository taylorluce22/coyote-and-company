## Sonoran Clinical Partners — Qualified buyers, delivered. You stay in control of every sale.

**What we do:** We build pipelines of license-verified clinical buyers — practices, clinics, and pharmacies in your product categories — and hand you warm, sales-ready introductions. You quote, price, contract, ship, and invoice directly. We never touch the product, the pricing, or the paper.

**Why it works for a 503B:**

- **You keep the relationship and the margin.** We are not a distributor or a middleman in the transaction. Every account we open is your direct account, on your terms, at your prices.
- **Every buyer is verified before you ever see them.** State license primary-source-verified with evidence on file, facility state checked against your licensure map, buyer type confirmed eligible for office-use purchasing. You get an introduction packet, not a cold lead.
- **Zero fixed sales-force cost.** No salaries, no territories to staff, no benefits. A monthly retainer plus a per-qualified-meeting fee, and a commission on collected revenue from accounts we introduce. If we don't produce, you're out a retainer — not a sales team.
- **Compliance-first by design.** All outreach is CAN-SPAM-compliant, claims-disciplined (no efficacy, equivalence, or approval representations — ever), fully logged, and stops instantly on opt-out. Our audit trail protects you as much as us.

**What an engagement looks like:**

| | |
|---|---|
| Month 1 | Target list built in your categories and licensed states; outreach live |
| Month 2+ | Qualified meetings delivered; introductions made; you quote directly |
| Ongoing | We track reorder cadence and keep accounts warm; you report closed orders and collections monthly |
