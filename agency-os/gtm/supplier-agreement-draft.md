**MARKETING AND INTRODUCTION SERVICES AGREEMENT**

This Agreement is entered into as of [DATE] (the "Effective Date") by and between [EXISTING LLC LEGAL NAME], an Arizona limited liability company doing business as Sonoran Clinical Partners ("Agency"), and [SUPPLIER LEGAL NAME], a [STATE] [ENTITY TYPE] ("Supplier").

**Recitals.** Supplier is an outsourcing facility registered with the U.S. Food and Drug Administration under section 503B of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act [adjust if other supplier type] that sells its products directly to licensed healthcare facilities and providers. Agency provides business-to-business marketing, buyer qualification, license-verification, and introduction services. The parties intend that Supplier is and remains the seller of record in every transaction with any purchaser, and that Agency at no time engages in the wholesale distribution, sale, or brokerage of any drug product.

**1. Services.** Agency will: (a) identify prospective institutional purchasers ("Prospects") for Supplier's product categories in the states listed in Schedule B; (b) qualify Prospects, including primary-source verification of licensure with evidence retained; (c) conduct commercial outreach to Prospects in compliance with Section 6; and (d) upon a Prospect's interest and Supplier's consent, introduce the Prospect to Supplier ("Introduction"). An "Introduced Account" is a Prospect so introduced, evidenced by Agency's introduction record.

**2. ⚑ Seller of Record; No Distribution Authority.**
(a) Supplier alone prepares and transmits all quotations, sets and approves all pricing, negotiates and executes all sales terms, accepts all orders, invoices all purchasers, and fulfills all orders.
(b) Agency shall not, and has no authority to: take title to or possession of any product; transmit pricing or quotations to any purchaser; accept, place, or direct any order; negotiate or contract for the sale of any product; or otherwise direct the sale or disposition of any product.
(c) Nothing in this Agreement constitutes Agency a wholesaler, wholesale distributor, broker, or sales agent with authority to bind Supplier. Agency is an independent contractor providing marketing and introduction services only.

**3. ⚑ Compensation.** As set out in Schedule A: (a) a monthly retainer of $[X], due in advance; (b) a fee of $[X] per Qualified Meeting (a scheduled meeting between Supplier and a Prospect introduced by Agency whose licensure Agency has verified as active, which meeting is actually held); and (c) a commission equal to [XX]% of Collected Revenue from Introduced Accounts, payable within [30] days after each calendar month based on Supplier's report under Section 5. "Collected Revenue" means amounts actually collected by Supplier from an Introduced Account for products sold during the [24] months following that account's first order. The compensation methodology in Schedule A is fixed for the Term and may be changed only by written amendment.

**4. ⚑ Federal Health Care Program Exclusion.**
(a) No commission or other compensation under this Agreement accrues on any product or sale that is billed or billable, in whole or in part, directly or indirectly, to Medicare, Medicaid, TRICARE, the Department of Veterans Affairs, or any other federal or state health care program.
(b) Supplier certifies with each monthly report that reported Collected Revenue includes no such sales, and shall exclude from every report any account or transaction it knows or reasonably should know involves such billing.
(c) Agency may audit records reasonably supporting the certifications. Any excluded revenue discovered after payment is retroactively deducted from the commission base.
(d) No amount payable under this Agreement is intended or calculated to induce referrals of items or services payable by any federal health care program.

**5. Supplier Reporting.** By the [10th] day of each month Supplier will report, in the format reasonably specified by Agency: quotations issued to Introduced Accounts and their status; orders from Introduced Accounts (reference, date, amount, status); and Collected Revenue by account. Supplier will respond to each Introduction within [2] business days and will give Agency notice within [2] business days of: any lapse, suspension, or discipline affecting a license or registration of Supplier; any FDA Form 483 with official action indicated, warning letter, or recall affecting products marketed under this Agreement; and any complaint or adverse-event report received concerning an Introduced Account.

**6. ⚑ Marketing Conduct.**
(a) Agency will market only to licensed institutional purchasers and their personnel acting in commercial roles. Agency will not: contact patients or consumers; market to prescribers in a manner intended to influence patient-specific clinical decisions; or provide anything of value to any purchaser's personnel.
(b) Agency's outbound materials will contain no claim of safety, efficacy, or clinical outcome for any compounded preparation; no statement or implication that any compounded preparation is FDA-approved or equivalent to an FDA-approved drug; and no product-specific representations other than those in Supplier-approved materials. Supplier has prior approval rights over product-specific copy and will ensure its approved materials comply with applicable law.
(c) Excluded products: this Agreement does not cover, and Agency will not market, (i) compounded semaglutide, tirzepatide, or liraglutide, or (ii) any substance labeled or positioned for research use only.
(d) No compensation accrues on business involving any laboratory, recovery home, or clinical treatment facility as defined in 18 U.S.C. § 220.
(e) Agency will comply with the CAN-SPAM Act and applicable marketing law in all outreach and will maintain and honor a master suppression list.

**7. Confidentiality; Non-Circumvention.** Each party will protect the other's non-public information with reasonable care and use it only for this Agreement. The identities of Prospects not yet introduced are Agency's confidential information. During the Term and for [12] months after, Supplier will not solicit business from a Prospect whose identity it learned from Agency other than through an Introduction, and will not engage a third party to do so. Introduced Accounts are jointly known; Supplier's direct relationship with them survives termination (subject to the commission tail in Section 3).

**8. Insurance; Indemnification.**
(a) Supplier will maintain product liability insurance of at least $[2,000,000] per occurrence naming Agency as additional insured, and provide certificates on request.
(b) Supplier will defend and indemnify Agency against third-party claims arising from: products (including their regulatory status, quality, labeling, and fulfillment); Supplier's sales practices; and content Supplier approved.
(c) Agency will defend and indemnify Supplier against third-party claims arising from Agency's violation of marketing law in its outreach or representations outside Supplier-approved content.

**9. Term; Termination.** Initial term of twelve (12) months from the Effective Date, renewing for successive 12-month terms unless either party gives [30] days' notice. After month [3], either party may terminate for convenience on [30] days' notice. Either party may terminate immediately for material breach uncured [10] business days after notice, or immediately if the other party loses a license or registration necessary to perform. Sections 3 (accrued amounts and tail), 4, 7, 8, and 11 survive.

**10. Compliance Cooperation.** Each party will maintain records sufficient to demonstrate compliance with this Agreement for [6] years and will notify the other promptly of any governmental inquiry concerning the arrangement. If a change in law or a determination by a state board or agency makes any part of this Agreement or the arrangement unlawful or licensable in a jurisdiction, the parties will suspend affected activities in that jurisdiction and negotiate a conforming amendment in good faith.

**11. General.** Independent contractors; no joint venture, employment, or agency (except the limited introduction function described here). Assignment only with consent. Notices in writing. Governing law: Arizona; venue [Maricopa County / arbitration per AAA commercial rules — pick one]. Entire agreement; amendments in writing. Counterparts and electronic signatures accepted.
