# Supplier Agreement — Term Sheet

Basis: `docs/10-regulatory-research.md` §5 (compensation contract terms) and §1 (activity discipline). This is the negotiating skeleton — the founder turns it into the signed agreement. Terms marked ⚑ are the load-bearing compliance terms: they are not negotiable, because they are what keeps the model license-free and the commission lawful.

## Parties & structure

| Term | Position |
|---|---|
| Parties | [Existing LLC] dba Sonoran Clinical Partners ("Agency") and [Supplier legal name] ("Supplier") |
| Services | B2B marketing, buyer qualification and license verification, and warm introductions of prospective institutional purchasers to Supplier |
| Term | 12 months, auto-renewing; either party may terminate on 30 days' notice after month 3 ⚑ *(≥1-year written term is a safe-harbor styling factor)* |
| Exclusivity | Agency: non-exclusive by default (may serve non-competing facilities). Supplier category exclusivity available as a negotiating concession in exchange for higher commission or longer commitment |

## ⚑ Seller-of-record (the licensing shield — verbatim concepts for the agreement)

1. Supplier is the seller-of-record for every sale. Supplier alone prepares and transmits quotes, sets and approves all pricing, negotiates and executes all sales contracts, invoices buyers, and fulfills orders.
2. Agency never takes title to or possession of any product, never transmits pricing or quotes to any buyer, never accepts or directs any order, and has no authority to bind Supplier or direct the sale or disposition of any product.
3. Nothing in the agreement constitutes Agency a distributor, wholesaler, broker, or agent with sales authority.

## ⚑ Federal-program exclusion (the AKS shield)

1. No commission accrues on any product or sale billed or billable, directly or indirectly, to Medicare, Medicaid, TRICARE, VA, or any other federal or state healthcare program.
2. Supplier certifies monthly that reported Collected Revenue contains no such sales; Agency has an audit right on the supporting records.
3. Any such revenue discovered is automatically carved out of the commission base retroactively.

## ⚑ Conduct limits (the case-law factors)

- Agency markets only to licensed institutional purchasers. No patient contact. No marketing to prescribers intended to influence patient-specific clinical decisions. No payments, gifts, or anything of value to any buyer's personnel.
- No commissionable business involving laboratories, recovery homes, or clinical treatment facilities (EKRA exclusion).
- Agency's outbound materials: no safety, efficacy, equivalence, or FDA-approval representations about any compounded preparation; product references limited to Supplier-approved factual descriptions. Supplier has approval rights over product-specific copy.
- Excluded products: no compounded GLP-1s (semaglutide, tirzepatide, liraglutide) and no "research use only" substances, in any channel.

## Supplier obligations

- Maintain all licenses/permits for every state it sells into; provide Agency current license list and licensure map, and notice of any lapse, discipline, recall, or warning letter within [2] business days.
- Report monthly: quotes issued to Introduced Accounts, orders, and Collected Revenue (basis for meeting fees and commission).
- Respond to Agency introductions within [2] business days (stale introductions burn buyer goodwill).
- Product liability insurance ≥ $[2]M with Agency named additional insured; certificate on file.

## Mutual

- Indemnification: Supplier indemnifies Agency for product liability, product legality/regulatory status, quality, fulfillment, and Supplier's marketing content; Agency indemnifies Supplier for Agency's outreach-law violations (CAN-SPAM etc.) and misrepresentations outside approved copy.
- Buyer data: buyer identities Agency develops are Agency's confidential information until introduced; post-introduction, shared for the engagement. Supplier may not circumvent (direct solicitation of Agency's un-introduced pipeline) during term + [12] months.
- No joint venture/employment; Agency is an independent contractor.
- Governing law: Arizona. Disputes: [arbitration/courts — founder's pick].

## Open items for negotiation per deal

Retainer amount vs. commission rate trade; category exclusivity; tail length (12–24 months); meeting-fee definition edge cases (no-shows, reschedules); minimum monthly introduction commitments (offer only after month 1 data).
