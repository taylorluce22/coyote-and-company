# Outreach Templates v1

Two audiences: (A) supplier recruitment (pitching 503Bs — this is selling *your service*, no drug claims involved), (B) buyer outreach (on behalf of a signed supplier — claims rules fully apply, G10 gates every send). All templates follow `docs/06-outreach.md`: real name, real business, postal address + one-click opt-out in footer, truthful subject lines, no efficacy/equivalence/approval claims. Merge fields in {braces} come only from the approved whitelist.

Standard footer (every message, both audiences):

> {sender_name} · [AGENCY] · [postal address]
> Don't want these emails? [Unsubscribe — one click]

## A. Supplier recruitment sequence (to 503B business development / owners)

**A1 — opener (day 0)**
Subject: `Buyer introductions for {facility_name} — you stay seller-of-record`

> Hi {first_name},
>
> I run [AGENCY], a B2B introduction firm. We build pipelines of license-verified clinical buyers — {category_examples: e.g., med spas, IV therapy clinics, men's health practices} — and hand facilities like {facility_name} warm introductions. You quote and invoice directly; we never touch product, pricing, or paper.
>
> Every buyer arrives pre-verified: state license primary-source-checked with evidence, state matched against your licensure map, buyer type confirmed for office-use purchasing.
>
> Commercials are retainer + per-meeting + commission on collected revenue — no fixed sales-force cost. We take a small number of non-competing facilities per category.
>
> Worth 20 minutes this week? {booking_link}

**A2 — follow-up (day 3): the diligence angle**
Subject: `Re: Buyer introductions for {facility_name}`

> Hi {first_name} — one detail that usually matters to compliance-minded facilities: our whole model is built so you keep control. We're not a distributor and hold no sales authority — our agreement warrants that you're seller-of-record on every transaction, commission excludes anything federally billable, and all outreach we run is logged, claims-disciplined, and opt-out-instant. Happy to send the term sheet ahead of a call.

**A3 — follow-up (day 7): proof-of-work angle**
Subject: `{state} buyer pipeline in {category}`

> Hi {first_name} — we're currently mapping {category} buyers in {state_list}. Before any introduction you'd receive a packet: entity, facility state, license verification evidence, and buyer-type confirmation. If {facility_name} sells into these states, the overlap is worth a look. 20 minutes: {booking_link}

**A4 — breakup (day 14)**
Subject: `Closing the loop`

> Hi {first_name} — I'll assume buyer introductions aren't a fit for {facility_name} right now and stop here. If that changes — or if there's a category you wish you had coverage in without hiring reps — my calendar link is below. Thanks for the time.

## B. Buyer outreach sequence (clinics/practices, on behalf of a signed supplier)

Rules in force: buyer must pass G1/G5/G9 before enrollment; product references are category-level and factual; **never** name-drop conditions, outcomes, or comparisons to branded drugs; supplier-specific copy requires supplier approval per the agreement.

**B1 — opener (day 0)**
Subject: `Sourcing {product_category} for {practice_name}`

> Hi {first_name},
>
> I'm {sender_name} with [AGENCY]. We connect {buyer_type_phrase: e.g., aesthetic practices} with FDA-registered 503B outsourcing facilities for {product_category: e.g., office-use sterile preparations} — facilities we've vetted for state licensure, inspection history, and insurance.
>
> If sourcing {product_category} is on your plate, I can introduce you directly to a facility licensed to ship to {state}. They quote you directly; there's no cost to your practice for the introduction.
>
> Worth a brief call? {booking_link}

**B2 — follow-up (day 4): the vetting angle**
Subject: `Re: Sourcing {product_category} for {practice_name}`

> Hi {first_name} — quick context on why practices use us rather than fielding supplier cold calls: we verify the facility side (state licenses, FDA registration status, inspection history, insurance) before we ever make an introduction, and we only work with facilities compounding under the 503B framework. One introduction, pre-vetted, no obligation.

**B3 — follow-up (day 9): specific and short**
Subject: `503B option for {state}`

> Hi {first_name} — one line: we work with an FDA-registered 503B facility licensed for {state} that supplies {product_category} to practices like {practice_name}. If your current sourcing is fine, ignore me. If it's a pain point, I'll set up a direct intro this week: {booking_link}

**B4 — breakup (day 16)**
Subject: `Last note from me`

> Hi {first_name} — I'll stop here. If {product_category} sourcing ever becomes a project, the introduction offer stands. Thanks for reading.

## Claims-linter notes for these templates (why the phrasing is the way it is)

- "FDA-registered 503B outsourcing facility" — TRUE and permitted (registration is a fact); never shorten to "FDA-approved facility" or attach "FDA-approved" to any product.
- Product references stay at category level ("office-use sterile preparations") — no drug names paired with outcomes, no condition/treatment language, no "same as / alternative to [brand]".
- The service being sold in every email is verification + introduction — a true statement about our own work, which is the safest thing to market.
- "No cost to your practice" is true (supplier pays us) — and disclosing supplier-paid status honestly if asked is mandatory. If a buyer asks "how do you get paid": "The facility pays us a marketing fee — your pricing comes from them directly, and we're not part of the transaction."
- New templates or edits = new version = CR + founder re-approval before first send (WF8). These are v1 pending that approval.
