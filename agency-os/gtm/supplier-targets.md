## Buyer-specialty demand (sequencing the buyer side once supplier #1 signs)

1. **Med spas / aesthetics** — ~11,500 US locations, ~15% growth; numbing/topical anesthetics (BLT) are staple office-use.
2. **IV hydration clinics** — ~$2.9B (2025) → ~$5B+ by 2030; chains scaling. Compliance nuance that *sells us*: NAD+/glutathione aren't on the 503B bulks list for office stock — a compliance-literate introducer is genuinely useful here.
3. **Men's health / ED clinics** — TRT ~$2.1B and growing; franchise expansion (e.g., Gameday) drives office-stock trimix demand.
4. **Ophthalmology practices/ASCs** — persistent shortages + ImprimisRx's CA exit = share shift; combo post-op drops are a real practice pain-killer.
5. **HRT/pellet clinics** — growing, but the big practitioner networks have captive suppliers; serve via independents (Belmar, Carie Boyd).

## Objections to expect from 503Bs (and the prepared answers)

| Objection | Answer |
|---|---|
| "Percentage marketers burned this industry" (TRICARE fraud era) | True — those were federal-payor, per-Rx schemes. Our model: cash-pay B2B only, commission contractually excludes anything federally billable, and we'll happily weight compensation toward retainer/per-meeting. Terms are in the term sheet. |
| "Are you a licensed distributor?" | We're not a distributor at all — you're seller-of-record on every sale; we never take title, possession, or direct a sale. The agreement warrants it. |
| "We can't sell to resellers" (503B wholesaling ban) | Exactly why we exist: every buyer is license-verified as a provider purchasing for own-patient office use, with evidence attached to the introduction packet. |
| "Third parties making claims scare us" (2025 WL wave) | Facility-approved messaging only, category-level copy, no efficacy/equivalence/approval language ever, full send logs you can audit. |
| "We're only licensed in X states" | We filter every introduction by your licensure map before you see it. Send us the map. |
